Our story

Funfinity is a women's fashion label from Egypt — modest, warm and made to be worn every day.

We started with one idea: abayas, kaftans and dresses that feel as good as they look. No stiff fabrics, no fussy fits — just soft chenille, velvet and heavy knits, cut for real life: work, family, winter evenings, and everything in between.

What we make

  • Abayas — the everyday layer, in velvet, crepe and more.
  • Kaftans — the statement piece, embroidered and unapologetic.
  • Dresses & sets — soft, strong, and easy to live in.
  • Hoodies & winter sets — cosy, oversized, and warm.

How we work

New styles land every week. Every piece is photographed as it is — what you see is what arrives. Prices are in Egyptian pounds, delivery is free over EGP 1,000, and you can pay cash on delivery.

Shipping & delivery

Fast delivery across Egypt — free on orders over EGP 1,000.

Delivery times

Orders placed before 2 PM ship the same day. Delivery usually takes 2–4 business days inside Cairo & Giza, and 3–5 business days to other governorates.

Delivery fees

  • Free — on all orders over EGP 1,000.
  • EGP 50 — standard delivery on orders under EGP 1,000.

Cash on delivery

You can pay in cash when your order arrives. Card payment on delivery is also available in Cairo & Giza.

Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll get a message with your tracking link. You can also ask us about your order any time through the contact page.

Returns & exchange

Wrong size? Changed your mind? Exchange it — no drama.

Exchange & returns

  • You have 14 days from delivery to exchange or return a piece.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, with tags attached and in the original packaging.
  • Sale items can be exchanged for size, but not returned for a refund.

How it works

Message us with your order number and the piece you'd like to exchange. We'll arrange a courier pickup and send your new size. The exchange delivery fee is EGP 50.

Refunds

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 3–5 business days of us receiving the return. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer or wallet credit — your choice.

Contact us

Questions about an order, a size, or a piece you love? Write to us — we reply within 24 hours.

How to measure

Measure over light clothing, tape flat but not tight. If you're between sizes, size up for abayas and kaftans — they're made to drape, not cling.
